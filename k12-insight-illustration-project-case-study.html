The Challenge vs. The Creative Direction

EdTech communications frequently lean on generic stock photography that fails to represent diverse student and educator experiences authentically.

The Challenge (Generic Imagery)

Standard corporate communication struggled with engagement barriers:

  • Impersonal stock assets that diluted unique brand personality.
  • Inconsistent art styles across web, presentation decks, and print.
  • Difficulty visualizing abstract educational concepts like school climate.
  • Visual fatigue from cold, purely corporate iconography.

The Creative Direction

Developing an expressive, custom-crafted visual language:

  • Character-Driven Narrative: Relatable, diverse representations of teachers and students.
  • Harmonized Palette: Warm, accessible color harmonies aligned with established brand guidelines.
  • Modular Scene Assets: Scalable vector compositions adaptable across web and print collateral.
  • Abstract Made Tangible: Metaphorical storytelling transforming survey data into friendly visual concepts.

Design System & Workflow

From initial thumbnail sketches to production-ready scalable vector assets.

1. Concept Sketching

Iterative ideation in Procreate exploring composition balance and metaphorical clarity before committing to final vectors.

2. Vector Precision

Translating approved sketches into clean vector paths in Adobe Illustrator, ensuring razor-sharp rendering at all resolutions.

3. Asset Integration

Organizing assets into structured component libraries for marketing and design teams to deploy across collateral.

Impact & Brand Alignment

Brand Recognition

Cross-Channel Consistency

Providing a modular asset toolkit ensured visual continuity across web pages, executive slide decks, and event marketing materials.
