Project Objectives & Brand Alignment

Translating complex educational technology and customer experience solutions into welcoming, human-centric visual art.

Humanizing EdTech Communication

Stock photography often feels detached and generic. The objective was to craft bespoke illustrations that communicate warmth, accessibility, and trust—reflecting K12 Insight's mission of connecting school districts with their communities.

Unified Visual Language

Developing a cohesive illustration style guide—defining line weights, color palettes, shading techniques, and character proportions that integrate seamlessly into existing digital and print collateral.

Creative Process & Methodologies

From initial concept sketches in Procreate to production-ready vector and raster assets.

1. Concept Sketching & Composition

Exploring narrative scenes in Procreate to establish balance and focal points. Sketches focused on depicting active, relatable interactions between educators, parents, and administrative leadership.

Initial Concept Sketches & Scene Layouts

2. Digital Rendering & Color Application

Refining line work and applying K12 Insight's core visual palette. Using subtle gradients, textured brushes, and soft shadows to give elements warmth and depth while maintaining high readability.

Color Palette & Rendered Asset Showcase

Final Deliverables & Use Cases

Multi-channel visual assets designed to scale across responsive web pages, presentation decks, and marketing campaigns.

Website & Landing Pages

Hero illustrations and section spot graphics designed to guide user focus down marketing pages and highlight key platform features.

Marketing Collateral

High-resolution raster art optimized for digital newsletters, downloadable PDF guides, and print media.

Social & Campaign Assets

Modular scene components that can be easily reformatted for social media graphics and presentation slide decks.
