Cinematography & On-Set Production

High-end video production requires meticulous control over lighting, composition, and sound to convey brand authority.

Lighting & Framing Discipline

Utilizing three-point lighting setups, controlled diffusion, and intentional depth-of-field separation to make corporate interview subjects look professional and engaging.

  • Precise key/fill light ratio balancing for modern corporate looks.
  • Controlled camera movement to establish dynamic visual rhythm.
  • Dual-channel lavalier and shotgun audio capture for crisp dialogue.

Narrative Storyboarding

Pre-visualizing key shot lists and B-roll coverage ensures the final edit tells a coherent story aligned with brand goals.

  • Detailed scene breakdown and pacing mapping before camera roll.
  • Intentional B-roll capture targeting authentic workplace interactions.
  • Multi-angle setup planning for seamless jump-cut-free editing.

Post-Production & Motion Pipeline

Transforming raw log footage into a polished, color-graded visual asset ready for multi-platform distribution.

1. DaVinci Color Grading

Applying custom LUTs and color correction passes to unify disparate camera angles into a rich, cinema-grade color profile.

2. Kinetic Motion Graphics

Building lower-thirds, title cards, and callout graphics in After Effects to reinforce messaging without cluttering the frame.

3. Audio Mastering

Dialogue cleanup, noise reduction, and ducking background score music to preserve vocal clarity throughout the narrative.
