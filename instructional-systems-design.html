Instructional Architecture & ADDIE Alignment

Bridging military training doctrine (Systems Approach to Training / TRNGCMD) with modern user-centered experience design.

Analysis & Objective Hierarchy

Training starts by defining the operational gap before authoring a single slide. Learning objectives are mapped in a strict hierarchical tree:

  • Terminal Learning Objectives (TLOs): Define the primary operational task the learner must execute independently.
  • Enabling Learning Objectives (ELOs): Scaffold foundational knowledge, procedures, and safety checks required to achieve the TLO.
  • Formative vs. Summative: Embedded micro-checks test ELOs progressively, while graded branching scenarios validate the overarching TLO.

UX Principles in Courseware

Applying digital product design to e-learning eliminates cognitive overload and maximizes learner retention:

  • Progressive Disclosure: Information is revealed contextually via modal layers rather than cluttering the screen with text walls.
  • Active Decision Branching: Replaces passive "click-next" reading with consequential decision loops that provide immediate corrective feedback.
  • High-Contrast Dark Canvas: Ergonomic, glare-resistant visual design optimized for varied field devices and low-light operations.
