Corporate Communication & Editorial Graphic

From Shelter to Stability: IAEM Bulletin Editorial

A full-page, information-dense infographic designed on behalf of National EMR for publication in the IAEM (International Association of Emergency Managers) Bulletin. Built to communicate complex emergency management workflows with visual clarity for industry leaders.

Client / Entity

National EMR / IAEM Bulletin

Publication Date

June 2025 (Vol. 42, No. 6)

Deliverable

Editorial Graphic & Infographic

Tools

Figma, Adobe Illustrator

Design Parameters & Objectives

Emergency management professionals receive vast amounts of raw data. The primary objective was balancing editorial depth with intuitive visual hierarchy.

Core Objective

Deliver a resource that translates complex response protocols into a high-retention visual layout that informs rather than purely promotes.

  • Outline key operational challenges National EMR solves in the field.
  • Illustrate a step-by-step roadmap of their emergency response process.
  • Highlight five direct, actionable tips from field experience.

Brand & Format Constraints

Specific spatial and visual parameters governed the execution:

  • Brand Palette Integration: Strict application of National EMR navy blue, red accents, and gold highlight hues.
  • Editorial Coexistence: Designed to function alongside a featured written article without crowding text margins.
  • High-Retention Hierarchy: Ensuring critical timeline steps remain legible at standard magazine print scale.

Drafts, Typography & Spatial Layout

Managing dense information requires treating text and layout components as fluid structural blocks.

Roadmap Architecture

The core challenge was accommodating a linear timeline within a fixed print aspect ratio. By establishing a minimum readable font size first, the roadmap was folded like a spring—flowing vertically top-to-bottom and stepping right into subsequent phases.

Roadmap Wireframe & Grid Layout

Text Fluidity & Spatial Hierarchy

The "Challenges" and "Field Tips" sections were formatted around the primary roadmap anchor. Strategic use of container padding, contrasting background panels, and gold accent rules guided the reader's eye seamlessly through secondary content blocks.

Draft Layout & Typography Scaling

Final Design Breakdown

How specific visual design decisions were applied to elevate information hierarchy.

1. Gold Focus System

National EMR’s gold hue was reserved exclusively for pivotal roadmap steps, drawing immediate visual attention to center-stage milestones.

2. Layered Depth & Contrast

A soft, blurred background graphic was paired with subtle card drop-shadows to establish clear foreground and background separation.

3. Specialized Iconography

Each of the five field tips features custom vector icons with navy and gold hues, providing visual shorthand without overwhelming the eye.
